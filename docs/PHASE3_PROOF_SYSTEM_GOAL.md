3. PHASE 3 PRODUCT OBJECTIVE
━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━

Build the real Q-HUB Proof system.

Routes:

/proof
/proof/[slug]

The Proof system must answer:

What happened when a technology actually entered the field?

It must NOT behave like:

- a case-studies marketing grid;
- a startup portfolio;
- an accelerator alumni wall;
- a collection of success stories;
- a blog;
- a logo directory;
- a generic CMS card archive.

The system should communicate:

FIELD CONDITION
→ TECHNOLOGY
→ ENVIRONMENT
→ TEST
→ EVIDENCE
→ DECISION / NEXT STEP where genuinely public

The existence of evidence does NOT imply commercial success.

A valid Proof record may eventually end in:

- evidence obtained;
- iteration;
- expanded testing;
- a documented next step;
- a commercial next step;
- a decision not to proceed;
- no publicly disclosed next step.

The architecture must not visually punish or hide a truthful non-success outcome.

Do not encode green = success / red = failure.

Evidence is the product.

Each semantic chapter should have one dominant job.

### FIELD CONDITION

Make the operating problem immediately legible.

One concise statement.

Do not restate the entire company story.

### TECHNOLOGY

Identify the introduced technology and startup.

Explain only what is necessary to understand what entered the field.

### ENVIRONMENT

Make reality visible.

Use approved environmental/field media and structured descriptors.

### TEST

This is the most physical chapter.

Use the existing approved Maradin test media.

Show test design / conditions without revealing protected KPI tables.

### EVIDENCE

Move from activity to documented observation.

Use approved stills.

Distinguish:

WHAT WAS TESTED

from:

WHAT EVIDENCE EXISTS.

Do not convert evidence into a success claim.

### DECISION / NEXT STEP

Render only fields that actually exist and are public.

For Maradin, render NEXT STEP.

Do not render a DECISION chapter.

Do not invent one.
