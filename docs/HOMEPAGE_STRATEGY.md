# QUANTUM IS THE PROTAGONIST

## Authority and durable hierarchy

This is the durable homepage strategy established by the human-authorized Phase R strategic reorientation. It supersedes the older method-heavy, single-POC homepage hierarchy only where that hierarchy concerns `/`. Phase 1–3 architecture, publication controls, Proof records, review evidence, and deeper Proof behavior remain historical and operative within their stated boundaries.

The editorial hierarchy is:

1. Presence
2. Startup desire
3. Partner access
4. General method
5. Broad activity
6. Evidence
7. Action

The implemented public choreography is intentionally:

`PRESENCE → ACCESS → STARTUP → METHOD → ACTIVITY → EVIDENCE → ACTION`

ACCESS appears before STARTUP on screen so the industrial ecosystem is felt before the startup proposition names what that access enables. This ordering does not demote startup desire: it makes partner power the evidence for that desire.

Future phases must not silently return the homepage to a project-first, method-first, or Proof-record-first hierarchy.

## Signature moments

Phase R concentrates authorship into three required signature moments plus one implemented fourth moment:

1. **Quantum Presence Reveal** — brand, type, negative space, and a restrained signal resolve with controlled, magnetic inevitability.
2. **Partner Field** — five approved organizations receive sole, borderless screen-scale focus in sequence instead of appearing as tiles, a marquee, a carousel, or a sponsor wall.
3. **Field Crossing** — a round technology signal materially deforms at a substantial threshold and resolves as a rectilinear field probe; large industrial planes replace repeating-grid decoration.
4. **Compressed FIND → TEST → PROVE** — distributed trajectories and selection become a hard contact boundary, then a paper-like evidence record and resolved check.

ACTIVITY adds an authored progression rather than another ambient loop: FIELD TESTING → PROGRAMS → PARTNER ENGAGEMENT → GLOBAL ECOSYSTEM exposes one contained signal at a time in normal motion, while reduced/no-JavaScript modes expand all four into readable flow.

Animation communicates hierarchy, access, transformation, and evidence. It is not decorative filler. Pointer response may enrich a moment, but no meaning waits for pointer precision, canvas, or animation.

## Content and presentation rules

- POCs support the brand; they do not define it.
- Proof is deeper intentional detail entered through `/proof/`.
- Homepage copy is intentionally sparse: one dominant statement, at most one short support line, and only essential relationship or category labels.
- Partner power is a core startup-facing value proposition.
- Relationship label → organization identity → scale/motion is the partner hierarchy.
- A small number of real approved identities is stronger than fabricated scale.
- Maradin and its record-specific facts remain absent from public homepage copy, metadata, and principal actions.
- The activity act implies momentum without publishing unsupported current-activity facts.
- The current homepage omits the older persistent textual phase rail; native scrolling, a restrained entry cue, and conventional site navigation carry orientation without permanent HUD copy.
- Conventional navigation remains usable; the homepage is not a miniature version of every supporting route.

## Mode parity

Desktop may add sparse pointer depth and the lazy custom WebGL signal layer. Mobile uses natural vertical progression, five non-overlapping full-width partner territories, and one contained Activity signal at a time; it does not emulate desktop pointer behavior and does not run WebGL by default. Reduced motion resolves each act into a strong static composition, keeps all five partners prominent (using typographic names when low-contrast artwork is unsuitable), and expands all four Activity signals into readable flow. Forced colors removes decorative layers and images while retaining headings, every partner name and relationship, all Activity labels, and working actions. The no-JavaScript and no-WebGL paths preserve the complete seven-act reading order in semantic HTML with DOM/CSS/SVG composition.

## Proof boundary

The Phase 3 Proof architecture stays deeper and intentionally more detailed. Phase R authorizes only narrow presentation cleanup: remove redundant Evidence copy, remove public explanations of withheld internal KPI material, reduce opening tag clutter where useful, repair header underlap, and send the homepage evidence handoff to `/proof/`. It does not authorize a second record, a changed Maradin factual boundary, a new outcome or decision, or altered eligibility.

## Release criterion

Automated publication, behavior, accessibility, bundle, and synthetic runtime checks are necessary. Human-perceived smoothness on the branch preview and human creative review are also release criteria. Synthetic performance or a Lighthouse result cannot by itself establish runtime acceptance.
